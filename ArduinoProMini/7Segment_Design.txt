For each of D3..D6 are INPUT_PULLUP
1. One side of a 2-pin header to the Arduino pin.
2. The other side to GND.
3. When the jumper is present (connecting pin -> GND),
   code reads LOW → interpreted as "1" after modifying readJumpers to invert logic.

Practical checklist to implement

1. For each input (D3,D4,D5,D6,D9,D10,D11,D12) place a 10 kΩ from pin to +5V on your PCB or breadboard.
    --> 10K from pin to +5V

2. Insert a 470 Ω series resistor between the MCU pin and the header/button (close to the MCU).
3. Optionally add 10 nF from pin to GND at the MCU.
4. Keep button wires short and use twisted pairs (signal + ground) if lengths are longer than ~10–20 cm.
5. Test with DEBUG serial logging on (you already added it) and exercise all buttons/jumpers while moving wires to verify stability.
6. If you still see noise, increase series resistor up to 1 kΩ or increase cap (0.01→0.1 µF) and retest.
